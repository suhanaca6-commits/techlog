Welcome, Fellow Scientist! 🔬

Have you ever wondered: "Is the position of my straw actually affecting my drinking experience?"

Probably not. But we're going to answer it anyway.

The Research Question:
What is the optimal position and angle of a drinking straw inside a cup to maximize the ease of drinking?

The Hypothesis 🤔

Some straw positions are more comfortable, efficient, and satisfying than others. We will prove this with science. (And data. Mostly data.)

What We're Testing 🧪

  • 7 straw positions: Centre, Left, Right, Front, Back, Diagonal, and Angled
  • Key measurements: Water drunk, time taken, attempts needed
  • Output: An "Ease of Drinking Score" for each position

Why Does This Matter? 🤷

It doesn't. But the data will be beautiful.
